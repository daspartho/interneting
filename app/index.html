a tool / toy for interneting

it needs a small add-on to work in your browser.

for now it works only in chromium browsers. please open it in one.

it needs the newer add-on: remove yours from chrome://extensions, then add this one.

download the add-on
  1. unzip it where it can stay.
  2. open chrome://extensions
  3. turn on developer mode.
  4. load unpacked: choose the folder.

then come back and it opens by itself.
